Colorful Robins

Pink Robin - (Petroica rodinogaster)

A small passerine bird native to southeastern Australia. Its natural habitats are cool temperate forests of far southeastern Australia. Like many brightly coloured robins of the family Petroicidae, it is sexually dimorphic.

Red-Capped Robin - (Petroica goodenovii)

A small passerine bird native to Australia. Found in drier regions across much of the continent, it inhabits scrub and open woodland. This species uses a variety of songs, and males generally sing to advertise territories and attract females.
